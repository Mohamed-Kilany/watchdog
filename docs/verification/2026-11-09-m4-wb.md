### AWB lock under a lighting change

Full run (torch on, locked): reported gains `r=2.0087890625`, `b=1.94140625`, drift 0.0.
Focused recheck with a persistent 10 Hz state stream and invalid-zero filtering:

```
locked base: {'r': 2.201171875, 'g_even': 1.0, 'g_odd': 1.0, 'b': 1.748046875}
torch on:    valid samples 9 of 37, drift 0.0, min == max for every component
torch off:   drift 0.0
unlock:      gains moved to {'r': 2.1552734375, 'b': 1.7919921875}
```

The A51 reports `COLOR_CORRECTION_GAINS` as all-zero for many frames while the torch
transitions (28 of 37 frames in the focused run). Those frames are not real gain readouts;
every non-zero sample while locked is bit-identical. The control path is therefore verified:
the lock freezes the gains and the unlock resumes AWB.

## Failures found (do not hide)

| # | Severity | Description | Issue link | Workaround |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | low | The A51 reports all-zero `COLOR_CORRECTION_GAINS` for many frames while the torch transitions (28 of 37 in the focused run). A naive drift check treats the zeros as real gains and fails. Every non-zero sample while locked is identical, so the lock itself is correct. | n/a (device readout quirk) | filter non-positive gain samples in tests; document the quirk |
| 2 | medium | `device pull` fails for every non-empty file with `sync chunk larger than the buffer we asked to fill` (reproduced with a 1.9 MB PNG and a 6-byte text file). The sync reader assumes each transport chunk fits the field being read. | [#22](https://github.com/Mohamed-Kilany/watchdog/issues/22) | base64 over `device shell` for verification evidence |
| 3 | low | A burst of one-shot `watchdog device …` commands makes the on-device panel flip between `connected` and `waiting for host`, and Android posts its `Connected to wireless debugging` system notification once per ADB connection. Normal streaming keeps one session open and does not repeat either. | n/a | persistent host session; PR #21 adds a 5 s status grace period for CLI bursts |

The transient `camera thread unavailable` replies seen in the first T06 attempt were a real
race in `runOnCameraThread` (the latch was counted down before the outcome was stored). Fixed
in PR #20 (`8dbda76`) and confirmed absent in this run (0 occurrences).
