The absolute luma differs because the TV content changed between the two captures; the
comparison that matters is stability. Consecutive-frame differences are dominated by the
playing video, and the row-banding index stayed low in both modes on this LCD panel.
The A51 descriptor advertises anti-banding modes [0, 3] only (OFF and AUTO), so fixed
50/60 Hz operation is not available on this device.

Moiré
The 2x centre crop (moire-crop-2x.jpg) shows the TV's RGB subpixel grid across the frame at
this distance and angle. A controlled angle A/B was not possible without physically moving
the phone; the guide's recommendation to angle the phone a few degrees remains the
mitigation.
